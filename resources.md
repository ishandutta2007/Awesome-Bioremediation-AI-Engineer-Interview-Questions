# Additional Resources

A short list of resources for going deeper on the intersection of AI/ML and environmental biotechnology. (Not exhaustive — PRs welcome.) This is a genuinely interdisciplinary field spanning microbiology, environmental engineering, synthetic biology, and machine learning — cross-check any specific degradation rate, cost, or technology-readiness claim against current literature before an interview.

## Foundational Reading
- Alexander, *Biodegradation and Bioremediation* — a foundational, comprehensive textbook covering the environmental science and microbiology fundamentals discussed in section 1.
- Tournier et al., "An engineered PET depolymerase to break down and recycle plastic bottles" (Nature, 2020) — a widely-cited example of the enzyme engineering approach discussed in section 2.
- Companion **Synthetic Biology Designer** repo — read first if genetic circuit design, the DBTL cycle, and biosafety fundamentals aren't already familiar, since this repo builds directly on them.
- Companion **Genomics Data Scientist** repo — read first if metagenomic sequencing and environmental sequencing data analysis fundamentals aren't already familiar.
- Companion **ML Engineer (Biotech)** repo — relevant for the production ML systems, monitoring, and data pipeline concepts discussed in sections 3, 5, and 6.

## Key Application Areas (good to be conversationally familiar with)
- PET and other plastic-degrading enzyme engineering — one of the most active, well-funded application areas in this space.
- Petroleum hydrocarbon bioremediation (a long-established, comparatively mature application area with substantial historical field-deployment experience to draw on).
- Heavy metal phytoremediation and bioremediation approaches.
- Emerging work on PFAS ("forever chemicals") degradation, a particularly challenging target given these compounds' chemical stability.

## Communities & Discussion
- The International Society of Environmental Biotechnology and similar professional/academic communities.
- r/environmental_science and r/synbio for broader discussion threads.
- Major environmental engineering and synthetic biology conferences increasingly feature dedicated sessions on this intersection.

## Companion Repos (adjacent, complementary content)
- **Synthetic Biology Designer** — genetic circuit design, DNA assembly, host/metabolic engineering, and biosafety fundamentals this repo builds directly on.
- **Genomics Data Scientist** — sequencing data analysis, statistical genomics, and NGS pipeline fundamentals relevant to environmental metagenomics.
- **Computational Biologist** — algorithms, statistical modeling, and reproducibility practices relevant to the modeling work discussed in sections 4 and 5.
- **ML Engineer (Biotech)** — production ML systems, data pipelines, and monitoring practices relevant to sections 3 and 6.
- **Engineered Living Materials** — relevant background on the real-world viability and deployment challenges of living systems outside controlled laboratory conditions, discussed here in section 6 in an environmental-release context.

## Staying Current
- Enzyme engineering for plastics degradation and PFAS remediation are both fast-moving areas — recent journal publications and preprints are far more reliable sources of current state-of-the-art performance than any static reference, including this repo.
- Environmental release regulatory frameworks vary significantly by jurisdiction and are actively evolving — check current guidance directly for anything specific to your work, rather than relying on this repo or older sources.
